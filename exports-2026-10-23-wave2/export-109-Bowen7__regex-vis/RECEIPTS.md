## Why the fix
- `src/routes.tsx` has `/`, `/samples` and `/playground` (dev only); see `router-check.txt`.
- With auto-detection and no config, `/samples` returns 404 on direct load or reload. The header links to it, so this is a real deployment defect.
- The `Anybuild` file was generated by `anybuild plan --wasmer` (v0.33.0) on this tree, so it carries the same detected settings. Two literal `[[advanced.rewrites]]` were then added: `/samples` and `/samples/` to `/index.html`.
